# Notice on linked content

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers the files in this repository only.

Handshake teaches American Sign Language by **linking** to lesson pages and
dictionary entries, never by copying them. Those videos, images, and lesson texts
belong to their creators — among them Lifeprint / ASL University (Dr. Bill Vicars),
Handspeak, SigningSavvy, and StartASL, all listed with their terms in
[curriculum/sources.md](curriculum/sources.md). None of that material is part of this
repository, is redistributed by it, or is covered by its license.

This is a deliberate design constraint, not an oversight. Those educators — most of
them Deaf — earn their living from their sites. Handshake sends traffic to them; it
does not mirror them. If you fork this project, keep it that way: `rules/tutor-rules.md`
Rule 2 is "link, never copy," and `tools/verify.py` enforces the allowlist that makes
it checkable.
