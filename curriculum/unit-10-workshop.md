# Unit 10 — Domain Vocabulary

## Why this unit

By now you and your partner can handle greetings, family, numbers, and daily
routines — but the specifics of your working hours are still missing, and your
working hours look nothing alike. This unit splits: everyone learns the same
shared base, then branches into the vocabulary of their own world, set once as
`track` in your `profile.md` — workshop or clinical. Learn your track and you can
tell your partner what actually happened today instead of reaching for the
nearest generic word.

set in `profile.md` — from the two subsections that follow. (Table rows run to
20 across both tracks, above the usual 8–15 guideline — that's the track-split
design, not an oversight: any one learner still only meets 14 signs, 8 shared
plus 6 from their own track.)

## Drill

When this unit opens, `/lesson` reads the learner's `track` from `profile.md` and
appends to the deck:

- One `vocab` card per shared-base row above, except FIX — seven cards every
  learner gets: "Sign: WORK", "Sign: MAKE", "Sign: BUILD", "Sign: IDEA",
  "Sign: PLAN", "Sign: PROBLEM", "Sign: MACHINE".
- One `fingerspell` card for FIX ("Fingerspell: FIX", source = the FIX page
  above) — the source teaches it primarily as a fingerspelled word (a signed
  version exists on the same page for later reference, once fingerspelling it
  is solid).
- One `vocab` card per row in the learner's own track table only — six more
  cards. Workshop learners get "Sign: COMPUTER" through "Sign: ELECTRICITY";
  clinical learners get "Sign: TEETH" through "Sign: PATIENT-(medical)". The
  other track's six signs do not enter the deck automatically — either learner
  can still add one as a `lookup` card if they want it.
- One `phrase` card: "Sign: WORK PROBLEM" (sources: the WORK and PROBLEM pages
  above) — the two-word combination you'll actually reach for when describing
  a bad day, whichever track you're on.

## Family mission

Pick one sign from your own track table that came up for real this week —
something that actually happened, not a random pick — and show it to a family
member along with what it means. Nothing from the track applied this week? Use
WORK + PROBLEM to describe any bad day instead; it works for either track.
Under 10 minutes, no rehearsal needed.

Learning with a partner? `learners/shared/missions.md` has the two-person
version of this and every other unit's mission.

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Sign the two-word phrase WORK PROBLEM, read back correctly by a partner
      without a repeat
- [ ] Read all 8 shared-base signs plus your 6 track signs correctly when a
      family member or partner signs them back in random order
