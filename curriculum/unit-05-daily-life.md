# Unit 05 — Daily Life

## Why this unit

Units 01 and 04 got you fingerspelling and counting; this unit is the first verb
set — WANT, NEED, EAT, DRINK, SLEEP, GO, COME — plus two rooms of the house you'll
actually need to name, HOME and BATHROOM. These are the words that carry an actual
request or an actual update about your day, and they're the ones a partner asks you
for constantly — "you hungry?", "you heading home?", "need to sleep?" After this
unit you can answer, or ask back, without reaching for the alphabet.

## Watch

- https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/lessons/lesson03.htm — ASL University's Lesson 3;
  new vocabulary includes WANT, NEED, GO, COME, and BATHROOM, used in real questions
  ("what city do you live in?", "is your house big?") rather than as a bare list
  (~10 min for this unit's five signs; unit 02 covers this same lesson's GOOD/BAD
  vocabulary separately).
- https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/lessons/lesson07.htm — Lesson 7, built around
  food vocabulary; walks through EAT and DRINK together in real sentences (~10 min
  for the EAT/DRINK portion; unit 08 revisits this lesson for the rest of the food
  vocabulary).
- https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/lessons/lesson05.htm — Lesson 5; introduces HOME
  alongside other around-the-house vocabulary (~15 min).

## Drill

When this unit opens, `/lesson` appends to the deck:

- One `vocab` card per Learn row above (prompts: "Sign: WANT", "Sign: NEED",
  "Sign: EAT", "Sign: DRINK", "Sign: SLEEP", "Sign: GO", "Sign: COME", "Sign: HOME",
  "Sign: BATHROOM"). 9 cards.
- Three `phrase` cards that sequence signs already in the deck — no new production,
  just order: "Sign: ME WANT EAT" (source: the WANT and EAT pages above, plus ME
  from unit 00), "Sign: ME NEED SLEEP" (source: the NEED and SLEEP pages above, plus
  ME from unit 00), and "Sign: ME GO HOME" (source: the GO and HOME pages above,
  plus ME from unit 00).

## Family mission

Sit down or video call with one family member. Take turns: they name something out
loud (hungry, thirsty, tired, heading out, heading home), you sign the matching
word from this unit with no voicing — WANT, NEED, EAT, DRINK, SLEEP, GO, COME,
HOME, BATHROOM, whatever fits. Five rounds each way, under 10 minutes total. This
is the first unit where your vocabulary can carry a real, unscripted answer instead
of a rehearsed one — see how far you get before anyone has to guess.

Learning with a partner? `learners/shared/missions.md` has the two-person version of
this and every other unit's mission.
