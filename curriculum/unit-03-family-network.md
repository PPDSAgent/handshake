# Unit 03 — The Family Network

## Why this unit

This is who the first three units were for. Fingerspelling (01) gets you through a
name; First Contact (02) gets you through the opening exchange; this unit gets you
through the sentence right after it — who someone is to you, and who you are to them.
These are also the signs that get reused the most: once FAMILY, MOTHER, or BROTHER
enter your hands, they show up in nearly every real conversation that follows, so the
return on four sessions here is higher than almost anywhere else in the curriculum.

## Drill

When this unit opens, `/lesson` appends to the deck:

- One `vocab` card per Learn row above (prompts: "Sign: FAMILY", "Sign: MOTHER",
  "Sign: FATHER", "Sign: WIFE", "Sign: HUSBAND", "Sign: SON", "Sign: DAUGHTER",
  "Sign: BROTHER", "Sign: SISTER", "Sign: GRANDMOTHER", "Sign: GRANDFATHER",
  "Sign: LOVE"). 12 cards.
- Two `phrase` cards. **These are fluency drills, not attested sentences.** Each is a
  run of signs you already know, produced back-to-back so the transitions stop being
  the slow part — the sources below teach each *sign*, and none of them attests this
  *order*. Real ASL sentence order is taught by the lesson videos in Watch and is not
  claimed here: "Sign: ME LOVE FAMILY" (sign sources: the FAMILY and LOVE pages above,
  plus ME from unit 00) and "Sign: MOTHER FATHER SISTER BROTHER" (sign sources: the
  MOTHER, FATHER, SISTER, and BROTHER pages above) — a fixed four-name chain everyone
  drills the same way, whether or not each relationship applies to them.

## Family mission

Video call, or sit down in person with one family member: sign FAMILY, then sign
each relationship word from this unit that describes someone in your life (skip the
ones that don't apply), then LOVE. Ask them to name back, out loud, which
relationships they recognized. This is the first unit where your new vocabulary
describes the actual person on the other end of the call — let that land. Under 10
minutes.

Learning with a partner? `learners/shared/missions.md` has the two-person version of
this and every other unit's mission.
